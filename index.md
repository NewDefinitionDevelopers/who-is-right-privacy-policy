This Privacy Policy explains how "Who is Right" ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum data necessary to operate the App and process purchases:

**Purchase and Subscription Data**
The App uses [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) to manage in-app purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat collects and processes:
- A unique app user ID (not linked to your real-world identity)
- Purchase and transaction history (product purchased, price, date, renewal status)
- Device and app metadata (device model, OS version, app version, locale)

This data is used solely to grant, manage, and restore your purchases/subscriptions across devices.

**App Integrity Data**
The App uses Firebase App Check (with Google Play Integrity) to verify that requests to our backend come from a genuine, unmodified copy of the App. This process does not collect personal information — it generates an attestation token used only for anti-fraud/anti-abuse purposes and is not used to track you.

**Information We Do Not Collect**
The App does not access your camera, location, contacts, or photos, and does not include any advertising or analytics tracking SDKs.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:
- Process and validate purchases and subscriptions
- Restore purchases when you reinstall the App or switch devices
- Prevent fraud and abuse
- Provide customer support if you contact us about a purchase issue

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

## 3. Third-Party Service Providers

We share data with the following service providers, who process it on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

| Provider | Purpose | Privacy Policy |
|---|---|---|
| RevenueCat | Subscription/purchase management | https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy |
| Google (Firebase App Check / Play Integrity) | App integrity verification | https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| Apple App Store / Google Play Store | Payment processing | Apple / Google privacy policies |

## 4. Data Retention

Purchase and subscription records are retained for as long as necessary to provide the service and comply with legal/accounting obligations, in line with RevenueCat's retention practices.

## 5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data associated with your account. Since purchase data is linked to an anonymous app user ID rather than your name or email, most requests should be directed to RevenueCat via their [privacy request process](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy), or to us at the contact below and we will assist.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
